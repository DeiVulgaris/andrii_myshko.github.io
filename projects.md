## Oracle / Pythia

**AI Oracle for uncertainty, reflection and decision-making**

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/DeiVulgaris/Oracle)

Oracle is an experimental AI system built around a simple interaction:

> **The Oracle gives the first answer. Your reaction gives the second.**

The system uses an Oracle-like interface to externalize uncertainty and observe the user's reaction to an answer.

The current implementation is a **working Telegram model**, not merely a conceptual prototype.

### Current stage

**Working Model → Product Development**

### Product directions

- AI Oracle interaction
- delegated uncertainty
- YES / NO / UNDEFINED responses
- reaction and resonance analysis
- conversational memory
- behavioral patterns
- personalization
- Resonance Threads
- AI-assisted reflection

### Looking for

- product entrepreneur
- technology partner
- mobile/web developer
- UX/product designer
- startup builder

## GloBell

**Location-based communication infrastructure**

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/DeiVulgaris/GloBell)

GloBell explores a different model of communication: instead of searching primarily for *people* or *services*, the user interacts with what is happening **around a location**.

The system is intended to connect people, businesses, services, requests and opportunities through location and real-time communication.

### Current stage

**Concept → Architectural Development**

### Core areas

- location-based communication
- real-time interaction
- discovery and matching
- local services
- requests and offers
- business participation
- network effects
- scalable infrastructure

### Looking for

- technology entrepreneur
- product founder
- CTO / technical partner
- startup builder

This is potentially a much larger venture than Oracle and would require an actual startup organization rather than individual development.

# How I Work

I am particularly interested in problems where the initial idea is not obvious but becomes visible through exploration, conceptualization and system design.

My typical process is:

**Discovery → Research → Concept → Architecture → Working Model → Product → Organization**

I am not looking to build every project personally from beginning to end.

The important point is to bring an idea far enough into reality that another person or team can take it further.

---

# The Handoff Principle

I have discovered that my strongest contribution to a project is usually at the stage where an unclear possibility becomes a clear system.

Once the architecture and core logic are established, the next challenge is often organizational rather than conceptual.

Therefore I am interested in a **handoff model**:

> **Create → Materialize → Build a Team → Handoff → Continue**

The objective is not to accumulate projects.

The objective is to create things that can continue to exist without requiring their original creator to remain the permanent operator.

Some projects may remain independent research or cultural projects.

Some may become products.

Some may require a startup and an autonomous team.

---

# What I Am Looking For

I am currently interested in meeting **product and technology entrepreneurs** who enjoy taking unusual early-stage ideas and turning them into functioning products.
