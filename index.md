## 🌟 The Core Idea: What is Metamonism?

**Metamonism** is a rigorous, non-speculative structural method in process ontology. It redefines reality not as a collection of static, isolated substances, but as a continuous, dynamic architecture of becoming. Existence is an interconnected flow of transitions. 

As a composer, I view **Cinematic Trailer Music** as the ultimate sonic and emotional expression of this philosophy. Music is pure processual reality captured in time. My latest album, *"Finish and Die"*, embodies this relentless, dramatic movement of existence.

## 💔 My Story: Creating in the Face of War

I am writing to you from Ukraine. Today, my country is enduring a devastating war. However, my most difficult personal battle is happening inside my home.

As an **internally displaced person (IDP)**, I was forced to flee my home due to the hostilities. *Official IDP Certificate: No. 2311-7501366823, issued on Oct 19, 2022.* 

Now, living in temporary conditions, I am the **sole, full-time caretaker for my helpless, elderly parents**. Because of this round-the-clock responsibility and our displacement, it is impossible for me to hold a regular academic, remote, or corporate job. 

Yet, the intellectual process never stops. In the quiet moments between my caretaking duties, relocation struggles, and the sound of air raid sirens, I continue to write my philosophical treatise, develop code for ontological frameworks, and compose epic music. It is what keeps my mind sharp and my soul alive.
