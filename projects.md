# PROJECTS & VENTURES

## Building ideas into independent projects

I am currently developing several independent projects at different stages — from working product models to large-scale technological and research concepts.

I am looking for **product and technology entrepreneurs, founders, engineers, researchers, and builders** who are interested in taking promising early-stage projects to the next level.

I am not looking for people simply to execute predefined tasks.

---

# 01 — ORACLE / PYTHIA

### A ritual interface for externalizing uncertainty

**Oracle** is an experimental product built around a simple idea:

> **Oracle gives the first answer. Your reaction gives the second.**

The system does not attempt to predict the future or make decisions for the user.

It introduces a minimal external perturbation — **YES / NO / UNDEFINED** — and allows the user to observe their own reaction.

The concept has already been implemented as a **working Telegram prototype**.

The product concept has been developed considerably beyond the current prototype, including:

- delegated uncertainty
- reaction and resonance
- optional memory
- behavioral patterns
- physical interaction signals
- AI reflection
- Resonance Threads
- personalization
- freemium monetization

### Current stage

**Working prototype → product development**

### Next challenge

Transform the working model into a polished, scalable product and test it with real users.

### Looking for

- product partner
- technology partner
- mobile / web developer
- UX / product designer
- entrepreneur interested in building a new consumer product

**Repository:**  
[GitHub — Oracle](https://github.com/DeiVulgaris/Oracle)

# 02 — GLOBELL

### Location-based communication infrastructure

**GloBell** is a larger-scale product concept for connecting:

**people · services · businesses · requests · opportunities**

in real time through location.

The underlying idea is to create a new layer of local interaction in which a user can discover not only places and businesses, but also **people, needs, offers, services and opportunities that exist around them at a particular moment.**

Unlike Oracle, GloBell represents a substantially larger technological and organizational challenge.

### Current stage

**Concept / architectural development**

### Development areas

- product architecture
- mobile / web infrastructure
- location services
- real-time communication
- matching and discovery
- business model
- network effects
- startup organization

### Looking for

- technology entrepreneur
- product founder
- CTO / technical partner
- startup builder
- people interested in creating a location-based platform from an early stage

**Repository:**  
[GitHub — GloBell](https://github.com/DeiVulgaris/GloBell)

# 03 — UFCPS

### Universal Framework for Complex Problem Solving

**UFCPS** is a research and technology project exploring a universal algorithmic core for complex problem solving.

The framework addresses:

- non-linear reasoning
- high-dimensional graph optimization
- complex constraint satisfaction
- recursive problem solving
- autonomous agentic systems
- system integrity and consistency

This project is different from the two product ventures above.

It is primarily a **research / technology platform** that may eventually provide infrastructure or intellectual property for future AI systems and applications.

### Current stage

**Research / architecture / experimental implementation**

### Looking for

- systems engineers
- agentic AI developers
- researchers interested in complex reasoning architectures

# HOW I WORK

My work is primarily at the intersection of:

**research → concept → system architecture → product logic → business model**

I develop ideas, formulate their architecture, build experimental implementations, and explore how they can become independent products or technological systems.

For implementation beyond the prototype stage, I prefer to build **situational teams around concrete projects** rather than create a permanent organization prematurely.

The composition of a team depends on the specific transition a project needs to make.

---

# THE HANDOFF PRINCIPLE

I do not intend to personally operate every project I create.

My goal is to bring a project to the point where another capable person or team can take responsibility for the next stage.

> **Create → Materialize → Build a Team → Handoff → Continue**

A successful project does not have to remain dependent on its original author.

It should become capable of developing beyond him.
